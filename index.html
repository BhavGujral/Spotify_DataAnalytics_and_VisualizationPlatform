PM Insight 01 · Discovery Ranking
Home Feed skips are nearly 2x Discover Weekly
Discover Weekly achieves a 17.8% skip rate (74.7% completion) vs. 34.6% on Home Feed—highlighting a clear win for re-ranking Home Feed cards with collaborative filtering.
PM Insight 02 · Monetization
India leads volume with 43.3% Premium mix
India accounts for 25.5% of total streams (2,551) yet trails UK (65.6%) and Germany (65.4%) in Premium share—making localized micro-plans the highest-leverage conversion bet.
PM Insight 03 · Retention
Smart Speakers & Podcasts anchor low-skip sessions
Podcast episodes and ambient genres (Lo-Fi & Chill) sustain above-average completion rates, creating high-retention hooks for cross-format recommendations.
